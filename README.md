<h1>The Smart-Invigilator: Revolutionizing Academic Integrity</h1>
<b>Executive Summary</b>
In an era of remote assessment, the bottleneck for educational institutions is no longer the exam itself, but the prohibitive cost and logistical nightmare of human proctoring. The Smart-Invigilator is a streamlined, automated solution designed to slash operational overhead while guaranteeing a 360-degree view of candidate behavior.<br>
<hr>
<b>The Value Proposition</b>

<b>Situation:</b>The transition to remote examinations forced institutions to choose between expensive third-party proctoring services or compromised exam integrity. Manual invigilation for hundreds of students simultaneously was both financially unsustainable and prone to human error.

<b>Task:</b> My objective was to engineer a lightweight, low-friction monitoring ecosystem that captures both the digital (screen) and physical (environment) workspace of the student without requiring expensive hardware or complex installations.

<b>Action: </b> I leveraged Python to architect a dual-stream recording engine. By integrating the student's webcam (mounted for a point-of-view perspective) with real-time screen capture, I ensured no blind spots remained. I automated the data pipeline by utilizing the Google Drive API, ensuring that upon completion, evidence is instantly encrypted, uploaded, and shared with the Controller of Examinations (COE) via a secure, unique link.

<b>Result:</b> This intervention reduced proctoring costs by 100% per exam session by eliminating the need for live human monitors. It further saved an average of 2 hours of administrative labor per student by automating the file management and distribution process.
<hr>
<h1>How It Works (For Non-Technical Stakeholders)</h1>
You don’t need to be a coder to deploy world-class security. The Smart-Invigilator is designed for plug-and-play efficiency:

Wear: The student wears a standard cap with a webcam attached—giving the institution a "bird's eye view" of the desk and hands.

Run: The student opens the application. It immediately begins capturing everything they see on screen and everything they do on their desk.

Submit: Once the exam is over, a single keystroke (q) triggers an automated sequence that saves the video, uploads it to a secure cloud vault, and notifies the authorities. No manual uploading required.

The Impact: Why This Matters
Total Transparency: Provides a synchronized record of the digital and physical environment, making "unverifiable" cheating a thing of the past.

Streamlined Auditing: The COE receives a clean, organized dashboard of links, turning a week-long verification process into a few hours of spot-checking.
